Rules and progress

How to Play Yakton Daily

Choose any puzzle mode and follow the instructions on its page. The Daily Challenge combines four short rounds and is the official way to record a daily streak.

When do puzzles reset?

Daily puzzles use Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) and reset at 00:00 UTC. Your local countdown shows the time remaining until that reset.

How do streaks work?

Complete the Daily Challenge once per UTC date. Completing consecutive dates extends your current streak; missing a date starts a new streak when you next complete a challenge. Best streaks and history are stored in this browser.

Does my progress sync?

No account is required, so progress stays in local browser storage and does not automatically sync to other devices. Clearing site data may erase it.

Can I share results?

Use the share button on a game page. It uses your device's share sheet when available and otherwise tries to copy a text result to the clipboard.
