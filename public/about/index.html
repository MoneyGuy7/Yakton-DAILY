About Yakton Daily

Yakton Daily is a lightweight daily puzzle platform built around original mini-games, predictable daily resets, and easy sharing.

Our principles

  • Play without making an account.
  • Keep daily puzzles consistent for everyone on the same date.
  • Use useful, direct instructions instead of fake popularity claims.
  • Respect privacy and reduced-motion preferences.
  • Prefer simple personal records over misleading, unverified leaderboards.

Yakton Daily is an independent project. No external popularity statistics or testimonials are claimed.
